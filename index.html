Sunset Bliss

A refreshing blend of orange juice, white rum, grenadine, and sparkling water, garnished with a slice of orange and a cherry.

2 oz Pineapple Juice

1.5 oz White or Coconut Rum

0.5 oz Grenadine Syrup

1 oz Mango Juice

Tropical Mango

A tropical blend of mango juice, white rum, coconut milk, and lime juice, served over ice and garnished with a mango slice.

4 oz Mango Puree

2 oz White Rum

0.5 oz Lime Juice

0.5 oz Simple Syrup

Strawberry Breeze

A tropical blend of strawberry juice, white rum, coconut milk, and lime juice, served over ice and garnished with a strawberry slice.

3-4 fresh Strawberries

2 oz vodka or White Rum

3 oz Coconut Milk

1 oz Lime Juice

Berry Blast

A vibrant mix of mixed berries, vodka, soda, and lemon juice served over ice and garnished with a berry skewer.

1 oz Raspberry liqueur

2 oz Vodka

1 oz Strawberry Lemonade

2 oz Lemon-Lime Soda
